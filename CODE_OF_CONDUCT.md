# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributions from anyone who wants agents in production to be verifiable. We ask for three things:

1. **Argue with evidence.** An oracle is accepted or refused on its bad case, its determinism and its usefulness — not on who proposed it.
2. **Be precise and kind.** Review the oracle, not the author. Explain what would make a rejected contribution acceptable.
3. **Respect the domain.** Sector gauges are written with people who know the documents. Their reference data and their real cases stay theirs; anonymize everything you share.

Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks are not tolerated. Report a concern to the maintainers privately (see GOVERNANCE.md); reports are handled confidentially.
